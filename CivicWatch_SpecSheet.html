What CivicWatch Does
📈
Congressional Trading Tracker
Real-time STOCK Act disclosure filings. Every trade your representatives file — searchable, filterable, and cross-referenced with their votes.
🗳️
Voting Record Database
Full voting history for every member of Congress. See how they voted on legislation that affected their investments.
💰
Wealth & Net Worth Timeline
Track how your representative's personal wealth has changed since taking office — sourced from FEC, OpenSecrets, and financial disclosures.
🤖
AI-Powered Analysis
Gemini AI synthesizes voting patterns, trade history, and campaign donors into plain-English analysis of each representative's priorities.
