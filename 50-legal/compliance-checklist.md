so a professional review is cheap and targeted rather than open-ended.

## Required artifacts
- [ ] Privacy Policy — accurate to actual behavior, not aspirational
- [ ] Terms of Service
- [ ] Cookie consent, if applicable
- [ ] DPA available for business customers
- [ ] Data deletion request path
- [ ] Data export path
- [ ] Subprocessor list

## App store specifics
- [ ] Data safety / privacy nutrition label matches reality
- [ ] Account deletion **inside the app** (both stores require it)
- [ ] Export compliance answered correctly
- [ ] Sensitive-category declarations (health, financial, political) reviewed
- [ ] Entity-vs-individual submission requirement checked
