# Social Media Plan — civicwatch

Organic social. Paid lives in `media-plan.md`.

The premise: profitable social is high-intent lead generation and authority
building, not follower count. Every metric below ties to revenue or it doesn't
get tracked.

## Audience
**Civic watchdog / accountability voter.** Decided 2026-09-04 (gantt #37).
Tracks a specific representative's trades for accountability, not personal
investing — they want to know *did my rep trade in a way that looks like a
conflict of interest*, not *how do I copy Congress's trades*. This reframes the
paid pitch: Pro's conflict-score / flagged-trades feature (see ADR-004) is the
proof point ("here's the data that shows it"), not an investing tool. Free
tracking/alerts is the top-of-funnel hook for this audience; Pro is "prove it."
Messaging should lead with accountability, not returns.

## What works now
- **Founder-led, human-first content** beats polished corporate output.
- **Video-first** — short form for reach, long form on YouTube as an evergreen
  search asset.
- **Repurpose** — one long asset (webinar, teardown, post) becomes a dozen
  short ones. This is the main budget multiplier.
- **Community participation** — answering real questions in niche communities
  builds more trust than broadcasting, and those threads rank.

## Community management
Social is two-way. Define response SLAs for comments, DMs, and mentions — an
unanswered question in public is worse than no post.
